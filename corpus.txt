Word similarity detection uses Word2Vec to learn relationships between words from their surrounding context.
The application supports a Continuous Bag of Words model and a Skip Gram model for training word vectors.
Cosine similarity compares word vectors and returns a score that describes how closely two words are related.
Top N similar words finds vocabulary terms whose learned vectors are close to the query word.
Sentence similarity represents a sentence by averaging the vectors of words known to the trained model.
Semantic search compares a query vector with sentences in this corpus and ranks the most relevant text.
Text preprocessing converts words to lowercase, removes punctuation, and filters common English stop words.
The Word2Vec vocabulary is learned from the training corpus, so a word must appear in the corpus to have a vector.
The visualization feature uses principal component analysis to show word vectors in two dimensions.
Training reads dataset corpus text and saves the trained CBOW and Skip Gram models in the model directory.
More documents can be added to this file, with one or more sentences per line, to expand semantic search.
